1. Project Overview & Main Purpose

NomReNom is a professional, real-life food ordering website. It works like popular apps such as GrabFood or Foodpanda, but specializes in fresh bakery surplus food rescue.

Every day, high-end bakeries produce top-quality fresh croissants, baguettes, sourdough loaves, and pastries. Items unsold by the afternoon are 100% fresh, safe, and delicious. NomReNom connects these bakeries with customers and university students who can purchase them in Snack Boxes (40,000 VND) and Meal Boxes (70,000 VND) at up to 60% discount.

Core Benefits:
  • For Customers: Enjoy high-quality bakery meals at very cheap, student-friendly prices.
  • For Bakeries: Recover ingredient costs from unsold daily items instead of throwing food away.
  • For the Community: Help achieve zero food waste and support environmental sustainability.

3. How the Website Works: Step-by-Step Flow

A. Customer Journey (Step-by-Step)

Step 1: Browse Food & Select Items
The customer opens the website, views delicious bakery items, checks original vs discounted prices, and clicks "Add to Cart".
Step 2: Check Shopping Cart
The customer clicks the Cart icon in the header. They can change quantities or remove items. The system automatically recalculates the total price in Vietnamese Dong (₫).
Step 3: Checkout (Registered or Guest)
The customer clicks "Proceed to Checkout". They can sign in with their real account, or simply enter their Name, Email, and Phone Number as a Guest without needing an account.
Step 4: Select Fulfillment & Payment
The customer chooses either Store Pickup (collect at bakery counter in 60 seconds) or Local Delivery (hand-delivered to door). Then they select a payment method: MoMo, VNPay, Bank Transfer, or Cash.
Step 5: Order Confirmation & Receipt
The system creates a unique Order Reference code (e.g., NRN-20261107-A646), deducts inventory from the bakery stock, and dispatches a detailed order receipt to the customer's email.
Step 6: Live 5-Stage Order Tracking
The customer visits the Track Order page and enters their Order Number or Phone Number. They see real-time updates as the bakery prepares their food.
Step 7: Pick Up & Leave a Review
When the order is completed, the customer enjoys their food and clicks "Leave a Review" to share feedback on freshness, taste, and bakery service.

B. Customer Real Email & Security Flow

In NomReNom, customers use their real email addresses and create their own secure passwords:

  • Account Creation: Customers register on /register.php. Their password is protected using one-way Bcrypt cryptographic hashing (password_hash).
  • Automated Welcome Email: When registered, the customer receives an instant Welcome Email explaining their account benefits.
  • Login Security Alert Email: Every time a customer signs in at /login.php, the system dispatches an automatic security email to their address with the exact date, time, browser, and IP address. If the customer did not sign in, they can click a button to reset their password immediately.
  • Email Dispatcher: Integrated with direct TLS SMTP (Gmail, Outlook, custom SMTP) and backed up to C:\xampp\htdocs\nomrenom\storage\logs\mail.log.

5. How the Database Tables Connect (Relationships)

The tables in nomrenom_db connect to each other logically to manage the entire food ordering process:

1. Bakeries & Products:

Each partner bakery in bakeries has multiple food items in food_boxes. Each food item also links to a category in categories.

2. Orders & Line Items:

When a customer orders, one record is created in reservations with a unique code like NRN-20261107-A646. The individual items ordered are stored in reservation_items, pointing back to the food boxes.

3. Customers & Users:

If the customer is signed in, the order links to users.id. If the customer used Guest Checkout, it links to guest_customers.id.

4. Payments:

Every order in reservations has a corresponding record in payments recording payment method, transaction status, and amount.

5. Reviews:

When a customer writes a review, it is saved in feedback, linked to their order and bakery, and instantly displayed on the frontend and admin panel.
