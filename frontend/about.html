About NomReNom

Rescue Imperfect Snacks, Save Money, Zero Waste

A pickup marketplace for near-expiry and damaged snacks (dented boxes, crumbled biscuits, melted-and-set chocolate). Partner stores list their stock, students reserve with VietQR, inspect at the store counter, and take it home — with zero shipping fees!

Our Story & Purpose

Eliminating Retail Snack Waste Across Campus Hubs

Every month, convenience stores and import snack retailers discard crates of perfectly edible snacks because of a crushed outer carton, crumbled biscuits, or a best-before date approaching in 2–4 weeks. These items are still factory-sealed, delicious, and safe — yet traditional retail systems cannot sell them at full price.

NomReNom bridges this gap. We created a transparent, student-centric marketplace where partner stores list their imperfect snacks at 40% to 70% off. Students reserve and pay securely via VietQR, visit the store counter near their university campus, inspect the item in person, and take it home.

Our Operating Pillars

Built on radical transparency, food safety standards, and counter inspection trust.

Real Stock Photos

No generic marketing renders. Every listing features actual photos of the specific item and packaging condition.

7 to 30 Days Left

Strict policy: nothing with fewer than 7 days left, and absolutely nothing expired. Inner seals must be 100% intact.

Zero Shipping

100% store pickup at convenience counters next to campus dorms. Collect in under 60 seconds with no delivery fees.

Inspect & Refund

Inspect at the counter before taking it home. If it doesn't match the photo, refuse it on your phone for a full refund.
