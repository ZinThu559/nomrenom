How NomReNom Works

A pickup marketplace for near-expiry and damaged snacks (dented boxes, crumbled biscuits, melted-and-set chocolate). Browse real photos, reserve with VietQR, inspect at the store counter, and take it home!

1

1. Browse Real Photos

Partner convenience stores upload real photos of each specific snack item, showing the exact packaging condition, printed expiry date, condition grade, and steep discount.

No generic stock photos: you see the exact defect before ordering.
2

2. Reserve & Pay VietQR

Lock in your snack item instantly by scanning the dynamic VietQR code with any Vietnamese banking app. You immediately receive a 6-digit digital pickup code.

Instant verification, zero payment fees, no double booking.
3

3. Check at the Store

Walk into the partner store counter near your campus. Show your pickup code and physically inspect the snack before accepting it.

Doesn't match photo? Tap "Refuse" on your phone for an immediate full refund!
4

4. Pick Up & Enjoy

Satisfied with the condition? Confirm the pickup and take your snack home! You just saved 40%–70% and helped eliminate food waste in Ho Chi Minh City.

No shipping: 100% store pickup means zero delivery delays and zero courier fees.
Our Strict Quality Code

What Stores Can List

We strictly distinguish between cosmetically imperfect snacks and unsafe food. Every item listed on NomReNom must meet these rigid criteria:

Near-Expiry Food (1 Month down to 7 Days Left)

Food with 1 month down to 7 days left before the printed expiry date. Completely fresh, safe, and packaged.

Dented or Damaged Packaging

Outer box corners squished from shipping pallets, crumpled cartons, or minor aesthetic creases while inner factory seal is 100% airtight.

Imperfect Shapes (Crumbled Biscuits & Melted Chocolate)

Wafers and cookies that cracked in transit, or chocolate bars that melted and solidified again in air-conditioned storage.

Strictly Prohibited on NomReNom

To safeguard student health, stores face permanent platform delisting if they attempt to list:

  • Nothing with fewer than 7 days left before printed date
  • Nothing expired. Period. Past-date items are banned automatically
  • No punctured or broken inner seals on food wraps
  • No homemade or unlabelled goods without factory packaging

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is there no delivery shipping?

Delivery shipping costs 25,000–35,000 ₫, which ruins the savings on a 15,000 ₫ snack! Furthermore, delicate chocolates melt and cookies crumble further inside courier bags. Counter pickup is free, fast, and right across your campus dorm.

How does the counter refund work?

When you arrive at the store counter, you inspect the item before taking it. If the dent is larger than shown, or the chocolate bloom looks unacceptable, simply tap "Refuse at Counter" on your order tracking page. Your money is refunded automatically to your bank account via VietQR.
